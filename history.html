The Story of El Roble Academy

El Roble Academy has grown throughout the years thanks to the dedication of its teachers, students, families, and staff.

Since its beginnings, the school has worked to provide students with knowledge, values, and opportunities to grow both academically and personally.

Today, El Roble Academy continues building its future while keeping the values and traditions that are an important part of its identity.

Important Moments

Some important moments in the history of El Roble Academy.

The beginning

In its early days, El Roble Academy was smaller; there weren't as many classrooms or a computer lab, and there were fewer students than there are now.

Growing as a School

Over time, more classes were introduced, and it grew through small changes.

New Opportunities

The school expanded significantly; a volleyball academy was established, and the team won several trophies. Mr. Diego introduced a muay thai class, a computer lab was set up, the staff grew, and new classrooms were built. The student body also increased, and students participated in competitions where they won many trophies; there is also a dance class where students showcase their dancing talent.

2026

El Roble Today

El Roble remains a great institution, even though its extracurricular classes are no longer the huge draw they once were and have scaled back; nevertheless, there have been several upgrades, such as the cafeteria, the playground, and the sports court, among other things.
