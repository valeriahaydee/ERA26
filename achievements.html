Our Achievements

At El Roble Academy, every achievement represents effort, dedication and teamwork from our students and school community.

A Tradition of Excellence

El Roble Academy is proud of the achievements obtained by its students throughout the years. Our school encourages students to participate in academic, artistic, cultural and sporting activities.


Every year, new students become part of the history of El Roble Academy and continue the tradition of effort and excellence. Over the years, El Roble has won various volleyball competitions—not to mention its talented students who have triumphed in taekwondo, dance, and science fairs, among other events—while also standing out for academic excellence driven by highly intelligent students.

A New Generation of Students

Every year, a new generation of students leaves their mark at El Roble Academy. Their dedication, participation and teamwork help make our school community stronger.
